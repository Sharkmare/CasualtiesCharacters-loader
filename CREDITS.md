# Credits

CasualtiesCharacters is a loader for character packs. It contains no third party art and no characters.

## Format knowledge

The skin folder layout a pack may use (a `Body/` folder of PNGs named after the game's sprites, 8 pixels per unit) is the layout used by the skin portal skin.cat-bot.de and by the ChangeSkin mod by 05126619z. No code from that mod is used; the loader was written from the format description.

## Other

Casualties: Unknown is made by its developers. BepInEx and Harmony are by their authors.
